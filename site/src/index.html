How it works

A minute a day. That's all.

Choose your rule

No drinks at all, staying within your own daily limit, or simply how the day felt. You decide what counts as a win.

Check in each day

Tap "It was a clear day", or log what you had from a list of everyday drinks. Add a note or a photo if you like.

Watch it grow

Your streak burns brighter and your tree grows. Milestones mark the first day, the first week, the first month, and on.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is sobr only for quitting completely?

No. You choose what counts as a win: no drinks at all, staying within a daily limit you set, or marking each day yourself. Change it any time in Settings.

What happens if I slip?

The day is marked as a slip, never a failure. If you have a freeze banked, it can protect your streak. If the streak ends, its tree is planted in your forest and a new seed goes in. Your clear days, all-time, never go down.

Does it cost anything?

sobr is free to use, with no ads.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android comes first. iPhone is planned once the Android app is settled.

Is this medical advice?

No. sobr is a habit companion, not a treatment. If you drink heavily and want to stop, talk to a doctor first, as stopping suddenly can be risky. If you're in crisis, contact local emergency services.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Your account → Delete account. You can download a backup first. Without the app, see delete your account.
